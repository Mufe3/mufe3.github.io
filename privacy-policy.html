Privacy Policy

Last Updated: November 20, 2026

Mufe3 Studio ("we", "us", or "our") respects the privacy of our mobile application users. This Privacy Policy outlines how we collect, handle, and safeguard your data when you use our Android applications.

1. Information We Collect

We believe in strict data minimization. Depending on the application, we may collect:

  • Aggregated and anonymous application usage statistics
  • Technical device information (hardware model, Android OS version) solely for crash diagnostics and performance optimization

Important: We do not collect personally identifiable information (such as your full name, personal email, address, or phone number) unless you explicitly and voluntarily reach out to us for customer support.

2. How We Use Information

Any collected information is utilized strictly to:

  • Enhance application stability, speed, and overall performance
  • Diagnose crashes, debug technical glitches, and release patches
  • Analyze general feature usage anonymously to plan future enhancements

3. Information Sharing & Disclosure

We do not sell, rent, lease, or trade your personal data to any third parties for advertising or commercial purposes. Information may only be disclosed if strictly mandated by applicable law or legitimate governmental requests.

4. Third-Party Services

Certain applications may leverage trusted third-party SDKs (such as Google Play Services, Firebase Analytics, or AdMob) which operate under their own distinct privacy policies. We encourage you to review their respective policies to understand how they process information.

5. Data Security

We employ standard technical and administrative safeguards designed to protect collected data from unauthorized access, loss, or misuse. However, please understand that no electronic storage or Internet transmission is 100% invulnerable.

6. Updates to This Policy

We may periodically update this Privacy Policy to reflect app updates and legal requirements. Significant revisions will be published on this page alongside a revised effective date. We recommend checking back periodically.

7. Contact Us

If you have any questions, suggestions, or privacy inquiries regarding this Privacy Policy, please feel free to contact us via email:

support@mufe3.dev
