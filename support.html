Frequently Asked Questions (FAQ)

Yes, all core utilities by Mufe3 Studio are completely free to download and use. Certain apps may offer optional one-time pro features or tipping jars to support ongoing development—without recurring subscriptions or invasive paywalls.

We engineer every app around strict Zero-Knowledge and Offline-First principles. Your notes, OCR transcripts, notification logs, and reflections are processed and stored locally on your device. We do not maintain remote tracking servers or sell data to advertisers.

Most Mufe3 Studio utilities (such as AksaraKu, Shadow Trail, Chameleon, NotiScan, and Prompt Notes) function 100% offline. Internet access is only required for initial Play Store installations or optional cloud sync if explicitly initiated by the user.

Our apps are optimized for modern Android smartphones and tablets running Android 8.0 (Oreo) through Android 15. The exact minimum compatible version for each app is specified on its Google Play Store listing.

Please email us at support@mufe3.dev including the app name in the subject line, your device model, Android OS version, and a brief description of the issue. You can also open discussions or issue reports on our GitHub page.

In-app purchases are linked permanently to your Google Play account. On a new device, ensure you are signed in with the same Google account used for purchase, open the app, and tap "Restore Purchases" in the app settings.
