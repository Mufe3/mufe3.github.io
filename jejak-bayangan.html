About the Application

An innovative reflection tool by Mufe3 Studio to understand the trajectory of your choices.

In an era where personal data is constantly harvested and commodified, Jejak Bayangan is built with unwavering commitment to user privacy and radical transparency. We believe your journal entries and personal reflections belong solely to you. Built as a calm and thoughtful daily companion, it lets you understand behavioral patterns without sacrificing peace of mind.

Key Features

Thoughtful tools crafted to protect your privacy and illuminate your personal growth.

Pattern Recognition

Log everyday decisions and discover underlying habits with smart, unobtrusive daily reflection prompts.

On-Device Security

All your notes, decisions, and history remain encrypted directly on your phone. Only you possess the key.

Stealth / Disguise Mode

Optional discreet app styling and biometric locks to guarantee that your journal remains confidential.

Clear PDF Export

Export weekly or monthly decision summaries into beautifully formatted PDF documents for offline reflection.

Our Privacy Promise

We do not sell, rent, or share your personal logs with any third parties. Jejak Bayangan operates on strict "Zero-Knowledge" architecture — not even the developers have access to your personal journal entries.
